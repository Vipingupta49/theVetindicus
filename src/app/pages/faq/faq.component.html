Answers to common pet care questions.

How often should my pet have a wellness exam?

Most adult pets benefit from a yearly exam, while senior pets or those with ongoing conditions may need more frequent visits.

When should I contact the clinic urgently?

If your pet is lethargic, struggling to breathe, has severe vomiting, or shows sudden distress, contact the clinic immediately.

Can I discuss nutrition and preventive care together?

Yes. We combine lifestyle, age, and medical history to help create a balanced wellness and nutrition plan.
